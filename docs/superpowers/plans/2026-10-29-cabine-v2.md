# A Cabine v2: bigger controls and deck panels

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Make every control on the board bigger and easier to hit on a phone, and use the empty space between the decks and the mixer for per-deck "laptop screen" panels.

**Why:** Mathias's first look on his iPhone (2026-10-29): too much dead room between the decks and the mixer, and the controls are too small to hit reliably. His words: "we absolutely must make the buttons bigger and easier to interact with along with new features."

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-a-cabine-design.html` (still the authority on behavior). This plan changes layout and adds display only: no control changes what it does.

## Global Constraints

- Every control stays in its FLX4 position relative to its neighbours (same order, same section, same rows). Drawn sizes may grow around their centre; hit zones may grow much further. The jog stays round, pads stay square.
- Two viewport targets for every measurement and test: **844 x 390** (installed home-screen app, safe areas 47 px each side, 21 px bottom) and **844 x 330** (Safari with its landscape bars, the case in Mathias's screenshot, same side insets).
- The crossfader hit zone still ends at least 34 px above the bottom edge.
- Board widgets keep pointer capture, multi-touch, pointercancel = release, and the existing action/binding contract (`renderBoard(root, controls, bindings, opts)`, `setLit`, `setLevel`, `notYet`).
- Everything from the v1 global constraints still applies: no phone-only gestures on the board, nothing on the board touches the network, no emojis or em dashes, no secrets or the friend's name, commits as Mathias, never push, bump `VERSION` + `SHELL_HASH` in `djlab/cabine_sw.js` once at the end.
- Dev: worktree `/home/ubuntu/dj-lab-cabine` on branch `feat/cabine-v2`; dev server `dj-lab-dev` at http://127.0.0.1:8137 (already running, `CABINE_FAKE_INTAKE=1`). Live (`/home/ubuntu/dj-lab`, container `dj-lab`) is untouched until the controller deploys.

## Review Focus
1. A tap in the space between two small controls must go to the nearer one, never to neither and never to a far one; a drag that starts on a knob's enlarged zone must behave exactly like a drag on the knob.
2. Enlarged zones must not steal touches from the jog ring or the faders (big continuous controls keep their full drawn area as their zone; zones only expand into empty space).
3. At 844 x 330 nothing is clipped and the panels still show BPM and the waveform.
4. The deck panel's waveform must be latency-compensated exactly like the v1 strip was (the playhead line marks what he hears).
5. The frame budget: two panels redrawing at 30 fps on a throttled CPU must stay under the v1 numbers.

### Task V1: Bigger board, bigger hit zones, bigger small buttons

**Files:** `djlab/static/cabine/ui/board-layout.js`, `ui/board.js`, `ui/controls.js`, `cabine.css`, `flx4-map.js` (only if per-control minimum sizes need a field), tests `test/board-layout.test.js` (or the existing layout tests), `test/controls.test.js`, `tests/e2e/test_board.py`.

**Requirements:**
- **Remove the top strip from the default layout.** New default layout `"panels"`: the board takes the full usable height (top inset 6 px, bottom per the crossfader rule). The three sections keep their internal proportions, scale uniformly to that height, and sit with deck 1 at the left edge, deck 2 at the right edge and the mixer centred; the two leftover gaps become **panel slots** (`board.panelSlots() -> [{x,y,w,h}, {x,y,w,h}]` in CSS px, exposed for Task V2). Keep `"spread"` and `"exact"` selectable (`?board=`) for comparison; `"panels"` is the default.
- **Hit zones.** Every interactive control gets a hit zone larger than its drawn box: start from the drawn box, grow it to at least 44 x 44 px (clamped to its section), and resolve overlaps between zones by nearest control centre (a Voronoi split inside the section). Large continuous controls (jog, channel faders, tempo faders, crossfader) keep their drawn box as their zone and are never shrunk by a neighbour's zone. Implementation choice is yours (one transparent hit element per control sized to its zone, or a section-level pointerdown that resolves the nearest control and forwards to its widget with capture); the widget behavior after the press must be identical.
- **Minimum drawn sizes.** Round buttons and small rectangular buttons draw at least 28 px on their short side (grow around their centre, never past half the distance to a neighbour's centre); knobs at least 30 px; pads keep their grid but fill the pad area with 3 px gaps. Legends scale with the control and never go below 6 px; where a legend cannot fit, abbreviate it the way the hardware does.
- **Report the numbers.** A table at both viewports: smallest hit zone, smallest drawn button, pad size, knob size, jog diameter, panel slot size, for `panels` vs `spread`.

**Tests:** unit: every control's zone >= 44 x 44 or explains why (clamped by section), zones do not overlap, big controls' zones equal their drawn boxes, `panelSlots()` returns two non-empty slots at both viewports; e2e (WebKit 844x390 and 844x330 with inset padding, Chromium 760x360): a tap 10 px outside every small button's drawn edge (toward empty space) hits that button; a tap exactly between two buttons hits the nearer; multi-touch SHIFT + pad + fader still works; nothing clipped (screenshots looked at). Screenshots to `/home/ubuntu/cabine-shots/v2/`.

### Task V2: Deck panels in the gaps

**Files:** new `djlab/static/cabine/ui/panel.js` (one `DeckPanel` per deck), `app.js`, `bindings.js` (only where the strip's API moves), `cabine.css`, reuse what can be reused from `ui/strip.js` (waveform tiles from peaks, beat/bar lines, hot cue markers, latency-compensated playhead, browse list); tests `test/panel.test.js`, `tests/e2e/test_play.py` updates.

**Requirements (per deck panel, sized to its slot):**
- **Vertical scrolling waveform** filling most of the slot height: time runs downward (rekordbox's vertical layout), playhead line fixed at about 30% from the top, beat ticks and taller bar lines from the grid, hot cue markers with their letter and colour, loop region tinted, latency-compensated exactly as the v1 strip (`posAt(currentTime - outputLatency - baseLatency)`).
- **Readouts above the waveform:** effective BPM (large, 2 decimals) with tempo % (signed, 1 decimal) and the range (±6/±10/±16/W), remaining time (-m:ss), key (analyzed, never shifted), SYNC / MASTER tag, loop length when a loop is active, and the **phase meter** (4 cells for the beat within the bar relative to the master deck; the current cell lit; both decks show it when a master exists).
- **A thin full-track overview** (vertical bar along the panel's outer edge) with the position.
- **Title**: shown on the panel (artist, title, ellipsised) and on a long press nothing (read-only, no gestures).
- **Moved from the old strip:** CAIXA button (top of deck 1's panel), REC button with elapsed time (top of deck 2's panel), Beat FX status (name, fraction, ON) in the mixer's FX column area or under deck 2's readouts (your call, must be readable), channel meters stay on the board.
- **BROWSE:** pressing BROWSE opens the track list as an overlay covering the mixer section (the board's laptop screen); BROWSE turn moves the highlight; LOAD 1/2 load; BROWSE press again or a load closes it. Same strip API names (`openBrowse`, `browseMove`, `load1`, `load2`, `closeBrowse`) so bindings and `flx4-map.js` `STRIP_ACTIONS` stay valid.
- **Read-only:** no pointer handlers on the panels except CAIXA and REC.
- Redraw at most 30 fps from the existing single rAF loop; panels stand down in portrait like the strip did; tiles pre-rendered once per load.

**Tests:** unit: phase meter cell for given positions and grids; readout formatting (tempo %, range, time); panel stands down when hidden. e2e: two tracks loaded and playing, both panels show BPM, key, time and a moving playhead; latency compensation (fake 0.2 s: playhead 0.2 s behind); BROWSE opens over the mixer and LOAD works; REC and CAIXA still work from their new homes; `test_ui_frame_budget` still passes at 4x throttle; screenshots at both viewports to `/home/ubuntu/cabine-shots/v2/`, looked at before reporting.
